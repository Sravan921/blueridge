Good to Know Before We Depart
  • Honest color report: best color is about 2,000–3,300 ft (French Broad, Mills River, US-276 gorge). Above 4,000 ft is past peak, with some bare trees.
  • Check closures at 6:30 am: NPS Parkway closures. The reported closure is north of our section (MP 344–355). I could not verify the live status, and some facilities close after Oct 31.
  • Pisgah Inn is closed for the season, so there are no restrooms or food on the ridge. Use the Visitor Center and Brevard.
  • Offline maps: signal drops between about MP 395 and 412. In Google Maps tap your profile, then Offline maps, and download Buncombe, Henderson and Transylvania counties.
  • Cutoffs: be off the Parkway by 4:30 pm. Home by 9:00 pm at the latest.
⚡ Tesla Supercharging (start at 100%)
  • Battery legs (approx.): Cary to Hickory ~175 mi, Hickory to Asheville ~70 mi, Parkway and US-276 loop ~100 mi, Asheville back to Cary ~240 mi. Every stop card shows its nearest charger and distance. Distances are my estimates, so confirm in the car.
  • Total day: about 550–600 miles with the Parkway and US-276 loop, so plan on 2 charging stops, maybe 3 in the cold. Ridges and heater use more energy, and the climb to 4,000+ ft costs range.
  • 1. Hickory (on the way out): Sheetz, 2191 13th Ave Dr SE, 8 stalls, up to 150 kW. About 3 hrs from Cary, and it fits our 9:15 rest stop. Charge 15 to 20 min.
  • 2. Asheville Outlets (best top-up): 800 Brevard Rd, 12 stalls, up to 250 kW, open 24/7. About 2 to 4 miles from the Parkway at NC-191, near the French Broad Overlook, so it is easy to use at 4:15 when we come off the Parkway.
  • Backup in Asheville: Biltmore Park, 264 Thetford St, 12 stalls, 250 kW. About 8 miles from the Parkway. The listing was not recently verified.
  • Hendersonville: 160 Coolridge St, 12 stalls, listed at 50 kW (an estimate). About 20 miles off the route and slow, so skip unless desperate.
  • Brevard: I did not find a confirmed Supercharger. Do not count on charging at lunch.
  • Return trip: charge to about 80% in Asheville, then top up at Hickory or Statesville (134 Venture Ln, 16 stalls, up to 325 kW, reported available Oct 30) if the car's trip planner says so. Each stop adds 15 to 30 min, so home could be nearer 8:45 or 9:00. Leave the Parkway by 4:15, not 4:30, if charging.
  • Before leaving: turn on the car's trip planner for Asheville, so it preconditions the battery. Check stall availability in the Tesla app, since I only had listings, not live status. Distances here are my estimates.
